## Study Techniques Included

- **Active Recall:** Test yourself without looking at notes
- **Spaced Repetition:** Review at intervals (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days)
- **Feynman Technique:** Explain concepts simply to find gaps
- **Interleaving:** Mix different topics while studying
- **Cornell Notes:** Organized 3-section note-taking
- **Pomodoro:** 25 min focused work, 5 min break
